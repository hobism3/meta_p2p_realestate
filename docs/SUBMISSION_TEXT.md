## Short description
Direct real-estate deals involve many intertwined terms—price, rental deposit, monthly rent, visits, handover, and move-in—negotiated through informal messages. DirectHome combines a property-discovery website and a private conversation room with two separate AI responsibilities. A simulated AI seller or landlord uses Meta Muse Spark to negotiate naturally. A neutral assistant, 집톡이, converts sourced conditions into structured, typed proposals; both sides must acknowledge the **same version**. Monthly rentals treat deposit and monthly rent as one bundle, while sale counteroffers remain negotiation history until there is a single final price. The prototype includes room-level authorization, server-side validation, proposal versioning, and deterministic integration/E2E tests. It does not claim real property verification, actual reservations, contracts, or payments.
